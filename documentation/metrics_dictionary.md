# Logistics Intelligence Metrics Dictionary

This dictionary defines every core operational metric calculated by the **Cascading Delay Intelligence** engine, including units of measurement, computational formulas, and business impact.

---

### Metric: Propagated Delay
* **Meaning:** The cumulative downstream delay imparted to connecting shipments across operational dependencies rather than by the original disruption.
* **Unit:** Minutes (or Hours)
* **Formula:** $\sum \text{propagated\_delay\_minutes}$ across all downstream echelons ($\text{cascade\_level} > 0$).
* **Why it matters:** Quantifies the delay amplification generated by the logistics network itself after the initial incident, exposing cross-dock dependency vulnerabilities.

---

### Metric: Initial Delay (Direct Delay)
* **Meaning:** The primary delay introduced directly onto the trigger shipment at the origin facility by the root disruption.
* **Unit:** Minutes
* **Formula:** $\text{direct\_delay\_minutes}$ of the level 0 root shipment event.
* **Why it matters:** Separates external shock severity from operational propagation efficiency.

---

### Metric: Total Cascade Delay
* **Meaning:** The total delay volume accumulated across all consignments involved in a cascade propagation tree.
* **Unit:** Minutes (or Hours)
* **Formula:** $\text{Initial Delay} + \text{Propagated Delay}$
* **Why it matters:** Represents the total network friction and labor/vehicle idle time caused by a single disruption incident.

---

### Metric: Cascade Depth (Echelon Level)
* **Meaning:** The maximum number of operational handovers through which a delay propagated from the origin trigger.
* **Unit:** Integer (Levels: 0, 1, 2, 3+)
* **Formula:** $\max(\text{cascade\_level})$ within a `cascade_id` tree.
* **Why it matters:** High cascade depth indicates systemic coupling where delays cross regional borders through multi-tier transfer networks.

---

### Metric: Blast Radius
* **Meaning:** The breadth of operational disruption measured by the count of distinct shipments, facilities, and customer deliveries affected.
* **Unit:** Entity Counts (Shipments, Facilities, Deliveries)
* **Formula:** Count of unique `affected_shipment_id`, `location_id`, and `delivery_id` per cascade.
* **Why it matters:** Identifies whether a delay is contained within a single regional corridor or spreading across the entire supply chain network.

---

### Metric: Delivery Buffer Consumed Percentage
* **Meaning:** The proportion of scheduled delivery slack buffer eroded by transit delays prior to doorstep arrival.
* **Unit:** Percentage (%)
* **Formula:** $\min\left(\frac{\text{accumulated\_delay\_minutes}}{\text{planned\_buffer\_minutes}} \times 100, 150\%\right)$
* **Why it matters:** Directly flags consignments on the verge of breaching promised customer SLAs before delivery failure occurs.

---

### Metric: Customer SLA Compliance Rate
* **Meaning:** The percentage of customer consignments delivered on or before the promised delivery window.
* **Unit:** Percentage (%)
* **Formula:** $\frac{\text{Delivered On Time Deliveries}}{\text{Total Deliveries}} \times 100$
* **Why it matters:** The primary customer satisfaction and contractual performance benchmark for logistics fulfillment.

---

### Metric: Deterministic Risk Score
* **Meaning:** A bounded multi-factor index quantifying the vulnerability of an active shipment to customer delivery failure.
* **Unit:** Bounded Index ($0.05$ to $1.00$)
* **Formula:** $(0.35 \times \text{Buffer Factor}) + (0.25 \times \text{Dependency Factor}) + (0.20 \times \text{Cascade Depth Factor}) + (0.20 \times \text{Priority \& Hub Factor})$
* **Why it matters:** Provides transparent, non-black-box prioritization for control tower dispatchers to intervene on high-vulnerability shipments.

---

### Metric: Bottleneck Index (Hub Bottleneck Score)
* **Meaning:** A multi-criteria operational score identifying logistics facilities that repeatedly create and propagate network delays.
* **Unit:** Score ($0$ to $100$)
* **Formula:** Weighted combination of departure delay rate (35%), cascade involvement count (30%), downstream propagated delay (20%), and dependency transfer volume (15%).
* **Why it matters:** Distinguishes genuine operational constraints from high-volume hubs that process large freight volumes smoothly without causing delays.

---

### Metric: Dependency Delay
* **Meaning:** The specific duration a downstream connecting shipment was forced to wait for an upstream feeder at a transfer facility.
* **Unit:** Minutes
* **Formula:** $\max(0, \text{actual\_dependency\_time} - \text{planned\_dependency\_time})$
* **Why it matters:** Pinpoints the exact cross-dock and shared-vehicle interfaces where transfer buffers failed.
